George Jones, first shopkeeper in Pascoe Vale, 1841

In our own words, George Jones Eatery

Paying tribute to the first business owner in the area, the original Mr Jones opened a general store years ago. George Jones espouses an ethos of simplicity, honesty and consistency.

We're an all-day eatery serving modern plates and great coffee.
